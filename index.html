How Service Finder Works

A seamless 6-step lifecycle connecting residents and providers

1

Provider Joins

Provider registers profile, pricing, and operational zones.

2

Task Posted

Resident creates requirement or selects direct provider.

3

Receive Bids

Nearby qualified providers offer competitive quotes.

4

Select Best

Resident compares ratings, reviews, and picks the provider.

5

Work Executed

Task completed professionally at the residence.

6

Review & Pay

Community ratings submitted and invoice settled.
